Chapter 01 · Origins

Rio de Janeiro, Brazil

English roots. Carioca instincts.

Born in the United Kingdom and raised in Rio de Janeiro, I grew up between two ways of seeing the world: the precision of one, and the warmth and improvisation of the other.

Rio taught me that the most interesting places sit where opposing forces meet. Mountain and ocean. Structure and freedom. That idea has followed me ever since.

Chapter 02 · London years

Sussex & London · 2001–2011

A decade in the engine room of modern media.

A degree in Multimedia & Digital Systems at Sussex, then live broadcasts at Bloomberg Television, the Shell.com rollout to 100 markets at Digitas, and campaigns for Volkswagen, Star Alliance and Yahoo at DDB, SapientNitro and AgencyRepublic.

With Bravo Media, between London and Rio, I led digital work for West End musicals like Billy Elliot and Hairspray. It is where I fell for design and user experience: making complex things simple, useful and beautiful for real people.

Chapter 03 · The entrepreneur

Santiago, Chile · 2016–2024

We build the bridge. You cross it.

In 2016 Start-Up Chile, Latin America's largest accelerator, backed my idea: The Bridge, a global network of digital professionals.

As founder and CEO from 2017, I grew it into a team of more than 300 people operating across Brazil, Mexico, Chile, Colombia and the USA, with clients like Uber, Walmart and Santander.

In 2023 The Bridge was acquired by Gi Group Holding, a €3 billion HR group.

Chapter 04 · The investor

London & Latin America · 2022–today

Where building meets capital.

Since 2026 I am Investor & Partner for Latin America at Gestio Capital, a London-based private wealth and technology investment firm backing category-defining companies in AI, deep tech and frontier innovation, such as Anthropic.

Before that I backed Brazilian founders as an angel with BR Angels, and studied private equity and venture capital at Bocconi. I look at companies the way a designer looks at a product: who is it for, does it solve something real, does it feel right?

Chapter 05 · Creativity & design

Design is how I think.

At Accenture Interactive I led a team of 25 UX, UI, research and design specialists across Argentina and Chile, winning more than US$15M in pitches. Before that came digital work for Coca-Cola, Yahoo, Nissan and Oi, a Cannes Lions shortlist and a seat on the El Ojo jury.

I taught digital business to senior executives at The Valley, and studied Designing and Building AI Products at MIT. The tools keep changing. The craft stays the same: curiosity, empathy and taste.

Chapter 06 · The wind

Preá, Ceará, Brazil

Then the wind changed direction.

Preá is a fishing village on Brazil's northeast coast, where dunes meet lagoons and the sea, and the trade winds blow for months on end.

Since 2025, as Chief Happiness Officer of Terra Ventos, I invest in seaside land and coastal projects along the Costa dos Ventos. Mornings on a kite, afternoons building, and a small farm where I raise chickens.

Technology, design and nature stopped feeling like opposites. All three ask the same thing: read the conditions, commit, adjust.

Chapter 07 · The human factor

Business is about people. And not only people.

Since 2022 I have been Board Advisor to Generation, the global NGO powered by McKinsey & Company. I helped launch its Chile office and grow it from zero to thousands of students, changing lives through technology education.

Closer to home, I support animal-welfare organisations such as SOS Animal Preá and Aruana Society, among others. Love for animals has always been part of how I live.

Technology scales. Care is what makes it worth scaling.
